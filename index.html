Awareness & record

The map of everything in motion.

Topography is the planning-state engine. At any moment, across every product, it knows what's active, what's drifted, and what the handoff says. The coherence score, the active-work view, the cross-session COMPACT record — all of it compressed into one durable surface that survives every session boundary.

The problem it solves

Every session starts from scratch. Until it doesn't.

Work across a complex portfolio is inherently fragmented — different products, different sessions, different agents, none of them holding the full picture. By the time you return to a product, the plan has drifted. Decisions made in last Tuesday's session are invisible to this one. Topography closes that gap: it scores each product's coherence, surfaces what's in flight across all of them, and writes a durable handoff record at session close so the next session opens with a real map, not a blank page.

Coherence isn't a feeling. It's a score you can compare to yesterday's score, and a handoff record that explains the delta.
How you use it

Three verbs. One complete picture.

score

Coherence snapshot

An axiom-weighted composite (0.0–1.0) for any product or scope. Run it daily; watch for drift. The score isn't decoration — it gates work that depends on a stable plan.

active

What's in flight

An aggregated view of open work across every observed product: intents accepted, signals unresolved, specs without a paired decision. The cross-product view no single product can produce for itself.

handoff

Session record

At the COMPACT boundary, Topography seals a structured handoff: what closed, what's still open, what the coherence score was when you left. The next session reads it and picks up exactly where you left off.

What makes it different

The COMPACT seam: where awareness becomes a plan.

The composition law the whole product is built around

Plan state is a projection, not a capture.

Topography doesn't store events — that's Loom's job at the awareness position. It doesn't store signals — those belong to each product's own .intent/. What Topography does is compress the outputs of the whole stack into a coherent plan-state record at the COMPACT boundary: Loom's selvedge comes in, a durable handoff goes out. The port contract is strict by design:

Plan state (read)

Coherence score + active-work view, derived from the landscape — never hand-entered, never fabricated.

Handoff record (write)

Sealed at COMPACT only. The engine writes; consumers read. No external write path exists.

Behind the veil

How Topography was forged.

Topography wasn't sketched on a whiteboard. It was extracted from a real failure: seven agents working in the same session without a shared plan surface, each producing outputs that drifted from each other by the end. The four convergences that named the same structural gap became SPEC-TOPO-001. The product was built to close them — and is governed by the same machinery as every other product in this portfolio.

The operating model

Intent

Topography carries its own .intent/ governance — signals, specs, decisions, traced in git. The 3-verb port contract is a ratified decision (TP-004); the COMPACT boundary is a tested seam. Topography was itself scored by its own coherence engine before it shipped.
